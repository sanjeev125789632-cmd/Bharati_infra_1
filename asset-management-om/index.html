Operations & Maintenance

Maximizing Energy Yield & Infrastructure Uptime Across India

Bharat Infra Associates delivers comprehensive solar & telecom O&M / asset management solutions to preserve asset value and maximize performance ratio (PR) for power plant asset owners and telecom tower operators.

Our specialized maintenance teams perform scheduled preventative servicing, robotic/manual module washing, drone thermal imaging, electrical insulation testing, and round-the-clock emergency repair dispatches.

Our Asset Management Scope

  • Solar PV Module Washing: De-mineralized water cleaning cycles, automated tractor/sprinkling washing, and dust/soiling loss reduction.
  • Thermal Imaging & Thermography: Infrared camera audits to detect hot spots, micro-cracks, bypassed diodes, and loose cable joints.
  • IV Curve Tracing & Insulation Testing: String performance verification, open circuit voltage checks, and earth pit resistance monitoring.
  • Telecom Site Preventative Maintenance: Tower structural torque check, guy wire tensioning, DG servicing, battery bank health test, and air conditioning maintenance.
