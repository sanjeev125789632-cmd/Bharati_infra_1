Mechanical Erection

Precision Structural Steel & Tower Rigging Services

Bharat Infra Associates specializes in structure & tower erection services in India. Steel structure alignment, verticality control, and bolt torque integrity are critical to project longevity. Our certified riggers and erection technicians deliver high-speed, zero-defect installation across complex site conditions.

From assembling fixed-tilt or single-axis tracker solar module mounting structures (MMS) to erecting 40m–60m telecom lattice towers and industrial steel sheds, we maintain strict safety compliance and structural tolerances.

Erection Service Offerings

  • Solar Module Mounting Structure (MMS): Column placement, rafter alignment, purlin fixing, and tilt angle calibration for ground-mount and rooftop solar.
  • Telecom Lattice Towers (GBT & RTT): Section-by-section derrick or crane erection, diagonal bracing, ladder fixing, and fall-arrest system setup.
  • Monopoles & High-Mast Lighting: Flange-connected monopoles, high-mast poles with motorized lowering mechanisms, and antenna mounts.
  • Torque Testing & Verticality Audits: Calibrated torque wrench tightening, anti-rust coating touchup, and optical total station alignment checks.
