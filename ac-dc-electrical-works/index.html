Electrical Engineering

Turnkey High-Voltage AC & DC Power Systems Integration

Bharat Infra Associates is an expert AC & DC electrical works contractor catering to renewable power projects, industrial installations, and telecom networks nationwide. Safe electrical design and fault-tolerant cabling are paramount to preventing power loss and fire hazards.

Our licensed electrical engineers handle complex high-voltage (11kV / 33kV) substation connections, DC string wiring up to 1500V, array junction boxes, distribution panels, grounding pits, and lightning protection systems.

Electrical Contracting Services

  • Solar DC Side Cabling: UV-stabilized 4sqmm/6sqmm/10sqmm DC solar cabling, MC4 crimping, string combiner box (SCB) termination, and voltage drop optimization.
  • AC Power & Transformer Installation: Inverter AC cable dressing, LT/HT switchgear panels, step-up transformers (11kV/33kV), vacuum circuit breakers (VCB), and RMUs.
  • Earthing Grids & Lightning Protection: Maintenance-free chemical gel earthing pits, copper/GI strip grounding mat, and ESE lightning arrestors.
  • Testing & Pre-Commissioning: Insulation resistance (Megger) testing, cable hi-pot testing, phase rotation check, transformer oil breakdown voltage (BDV) test.
