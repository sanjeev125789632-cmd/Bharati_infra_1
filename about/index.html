About Bharat Infra Associates

Established 2021

Pan-India EPC Execution Powered by Dedicated In-House Crews

Founded in 2021 in Delhi, Bharat Infra Associates has rapidly grown into one of India’s most dependable Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) contractors for solar energy, telecom infrastructure, heavy civil works, and high-voltage electrical installations.

We operate on a simple core principle: Direct accountability through in-house technical execution. Rather than relying on multi-tiered subcontracting, we deploy our own trained engineers, riggers, heavy machinery operators, and electrical specialists to every job site across India.

Having successfully completed over 100+ projects for Tier-1 infrastructure developers, state utilities, and telecom tower companies, we continue to set benchmarks in speed, safety, and structural integrity.
