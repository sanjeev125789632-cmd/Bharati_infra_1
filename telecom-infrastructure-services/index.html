Telecom EPC Solutions

Turnkey Cell Site Civil, Tower Erection & Network Infrastructure

Bharat Infra Associates is a leading telecom infrastructure contractor in India, offering comprehensive EPC services for telecommunication tower companies, mobile network operators (IP-1), and telecom equipment manufacturers.

Operating nationwide since 2021, our skilled engineering crews specialize in Ground Based Tower (GBT) raft foundations, Roof Top Tower (RTT) beam strengthening, lattice tower assembly, optical fiber trenching, generator set plinths, and electrical grid connection.

Our Telecom Infrastructure Capabilities

  • Ground Based Towers (GBT): Deep foundation excavation, rebar tying, concrete pouring, stub setting, and lattice tower bolt torqueing up to 60m height.
  • Roof Top Towers (RTT) & Monopoles: Structural load calculation, structural steel beam retrofitting, anchor bolting, and rooftop mast placement.
  • Optical Fiber Cable (OFC) Laying: HDD trenchless drilling, manual excavation, HDPE pipe ducting, manhole construction, and fiber blowing.
  • Cell Site Electrification & Civil: Shelter placement, DG pad construction, boundary wall/chain-link fencing, transformer installation, and earthing grid connection.
