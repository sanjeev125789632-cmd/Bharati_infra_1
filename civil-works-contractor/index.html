Heavy Civil Engineering

Precision Civil Engineering & Site Infrastructure Contracting

Bharat Infra Associates is a trusted civil works contractor for solar and telecom projects. With specialized earthmoving equipment, concrete batching standards, and experienced site supervisors, we handle tough terrain and demanding project schedules with total quality control.

Our civil contracting team undertakes everything from initial site clearing and levelling to heavy RCC foundations, transformer yards, cable trenches, invertor room civil structures, and storm water management systems.

Our Core Civil Contracting Solutions

  • Equipment Plinths & Foundation Pads: Reinforced cement concrete (RCC) foundations for inverter stations, switchgears, and power transformers.
  • Cable Trenching & Ducting: Earth excavation, brick-lined or precast concrete cable trenches, sand cushioning, and DWC pipe ducting.
  • Site Preparation & Grading: Topsoil stripping, land levelling, compaction, and internal access road construction with gravel surfacing.
  • Perimeter Fencing & Retaining Walls: Precast RCC boundary walls, chain-link security fencing, anti-climb barriers, and retaining walls.
