Solar Energy EPC

Turnkey Commercial, Industrial & Utility-Scale Solar EPC Contracting

As a premier solar EPC contractor in Delhi NCR, Bharat Infra Associates provides end-to-end solar engineering, procurement, and construction services. Operating since 2021, our specialized engineers deliver custom-engineered rooftop solar installations and multi-megawatt ground-mounted solar power plants across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, and pan-India.

We combine precision structural engineering with high-efficiency PV module mounting, robust DC string cabling, grid-tied inverter commissioning, and seamless DISCOM net metering approvals.

Our Core Solar EPC Capabilities

  • Rooftop Solar EPC: Commercial & industrial roof assessment, ballast & anchored structure design, module installation, zero roof penetration options.
  • Ground-Mounted Solar Plants: Land contouring, piling/ramming foundations, MMS steel assembly, central inverter stations, and switchyard cabling.
  • DISCOM Liaisoning: Complete documentation, safety inspector approvals, meter testing, and net metering synchronization with BSES, TPDDL, DHBVN, UPVCL.
  • Procurement & Supply Chain: Tier-1 ALMM approved solar modules, string inverters, galvanised steel structures, and UV-resistant solar cables.
